New glyphs

h is 16 points, a is 15. Both constructed rather than traced — every point on an extremum, handles at the circle constant. No solver places a control point, so a lump cannot occur.

Built per weight

h, a and exclam are generated from the stem width rather than offset from the Black master, so their terminals stay round instead of collapsing to spikes. The g is still offset, which is why it narrows across the ladder.

Spacing is provisional

Sidebearings are 47 units opening to 58 at Thin, taken from the logotype's average gap. Real spacing needs fitting by eye.
